    Weekly Device Sweeps¶

    Every week the catalogue gets a short sweep: a few devices from the backlog become validated specs, and the news since the last sweep is searched for devices that are newly dying (cloud shutdowns, bankruptcies, withdrawn apps, new paywalls, firmware that removes local access) or newly popular, where local control is reasonably achievable. The backlog is Missing Devices; this page is the dated log on top of it.

    Like the survey, each entry is a snapshot. Grep device-specs/devices/, research-notes/ and targets/ before starting on anything listed here. Claims marked unverified rest on a search snippet or a page that would not load, and need checking before they go into a spec.

    Status changes to items already listed¶

    • Neato Botvac: heise reported the cloud phase-out in the week of 2025-10-06 (heise), earlier than the survey's late-December date. A Vorwerk exchange programme from 2026-04-24 is unverified.
    • Melnor RainCloud: Home Assistant 2026.10 removed the integration because the cloud no longer responds (release notes), confirming the survey's April 2026 date.
    • SolarEdge: the same release dropped SolarEdge Local and added a core SolarEdge Modbus integration, which backs the survey's "Modbus TCP is the durable path". The entry should be re-scoped to Modbus.
    • Tesla Powerwall: a 2026-09-17 community report says local interfaces were disabled on the customer Wi-Fi after a Powerwall 2 to 3 swap (HA community); firmware version not given.
    • Sleep Number: the sale to Sleep Country Canada closed 2026-07-31 (Davis Polk); the new owner's plans for the SleepIQ cloud are unknown.
    • Owlet Smart Sock 2: Owlet's own page now confirms the 2025-04-08 cutoff (Owlet support).
    • Wallbox: the restructuring plan became final in July 2026 after a new equity raise, easing the going-concern risk the survey cites.
    • Sense monitor (research-notes/sense-monitor.yaml): Sense stopped selling the consumer monitor on 2025-12-31 (secondary source); risk moves to B. Still cloud-only.
    • TiVo: free SkipMode reportedly ends 2026-11-02 in favour of a paid add-on (unverified); the network remote is unaffected.
    • Enphase Envoy: a June 2026 community thread reports /api/v1/production stalling on firmware D8.3.5528 (unverified, page did not load). The spec was observed on D8.3.5427; check on the next verification session.
    • Accell group (Haibike, Ghost, Raleigh, Lapierre, Winora) was declared bankrupt on 2026-08-11. Their bikes use Bosch, Yamaha or Shimano drives, so the drive specs are the relevant coverage.

    No local path¶

    Samsung's Korea-only SmartThings Tracker (service ends 2026-10-31), the Limitless pendant (Meta acquisition; no public protocol work), Meta Ray-Ban's paywalled Conversation Focus, Permobil wheelchairs (cloud API deprecated), Hookii Neomow mowers (vulnerabilities only, no supported path), Spectrum Home Security and Rad Power Bikes. Senec home batteries are a lead worth a targeted look: EnBW is winding down new sales, and a community LAN API on Senec.Home V3 is unverified.
