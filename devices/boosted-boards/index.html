    Boosted Boards and Boosted Rev (app BLE profile)¶

    Status: Spec Available (static analysis of the last vendor Android app; no capture, not run against hardware) Protocol: BLE Manufacturer: Boosted Inc. (shut down 2020; the remaining inventory was bought by Boosted USA, a separate company) Manufacturer Status: Shutdown

    Overview¶

    Boosted made electric skateboards: the 1st-gen Single, Dual and Dual+, the 2nd-gen Dual and Dual+, the 3rd-gen Plus and Stealth, and the Mini S and Mini X. It also made the Boosted Rev scooter. The company stopped trading in 2020, its app is gone from the stores, and its servers are offline. Owners still ride these boards in large numbers.

    A board has two radio links. The handheld remote drives the board over its own BLE link, and that riding link is out of scope here. The community has documented it (see References). This page covers the other link, phone to board. The vendor app used it to show speed, odometer, battery and charging state, pick a ride mode, rename the board, set display units and drive the lights. That link is plain GATT straight from the phone and never needed the cloud. The cloud handled accounts, ride logs, the inbox and firmware hosting, so everything on this page still works with the servers gone.

    The machine-readable spec is device-specs/devices/boosted-boards.yaml. Every fact in it is verification: reported. The characteristics, value layouts and writes come from static analysis of the final Android release of the vendor app (1.4.5, December 2019). Two placements are backed by owners reading real hardware with a generic BLE explorer, as reported in the Forever Boosted FAQ: the Device Information service and the battery service UUID. Nobody on this project has connected to a board.

    A ride-mode write changes a moving vehicle

    These boards reach about 38 km/h. A ride-mode write changes acceleration and top speed the moment it lands. The spec documents only what the vendor app did, which is picking one of the firmware's own modes, and marks it advanced. Send it only with the board stopped and nobody on it. Speed-cap changes, battery limits and the firmware-flashing protocol are deliberately left out.

    In scope: the phone-app GATT profile of 2nd/3rd-gen boards and the Rev. The 1st-gen boards are identified here, but their serial payload is not mapped.

    Out of scope: the remote-to-board riding link; firmware and bootloader frames on the serial characteristics; anything beyond the firmware's own ride modes; and the dead cloud API.

    Hardware¶

    Property Value
    Models 2nd gen: Dual, Dual+ · 3rd gen: Plus, Stealth · Mini S, Mini X · Boosted Rev scooter · 1st gen (identification only): Single, Dual, Dual+
    Model codes (7dc59643) 1 Dual, 2 Dual+, 3 Plus, 4 Stealth, 5 Mini S, 6 Mini X, 7 Rev, 0 unknown
    Chipset Not recorded for 2nd/3rd gen. The 1st-gen boards use a BlueRadios module running its BRSP serial-port service
    Radio Bluetooth LE: the phone link here, plus a separate BLE link to the remote
    Last firmware ESC 2.7.2 and extended-range battery 2.5.1, per the owner community. Official images are no longer downloadable
    FCC ID Not harvested. No Boosted grantee turns up on fccid.co, so the boards probably rely on module grants

    Initial Setup¶

    Property Value
    Setup required No. Boards ride out of the box with their remote. The phone link needs only a bond
    Method ble_direct (primary). The vendor account and registration route is historical, because its servers are gone
    Advertised service 7dc55a86-c61f-11e5-9912-ba0be0483c18 (the app's own scan filter)
    Advertised name The owner-editable board name, so not a matcher
    Passphrase protection not_applicable
    Confidence medium (vendor support article plus the app's code; not replayed)

    On every connection the vendor app did the following:

    1. Scan in low-latency mode for 7dc55a86-c61f-11e5-9912-ba0be0483c18. Boards in pairing mode are listed first. Pairing mode is bit 0x02 of the first manufacturer-data byte.
    2. If the phone holds no bond, ask the OS to bond and wait. The app never opens GATT to an unbonded board. On boards whose advertised firmware is older than v2.0.0, it deleted the phone's bond first and bonded again every time.
    3. Connect, with a 45 s timeout. Read every characteristic it knows that the GATT table actually contains.
    4. Depending on the Firmware Revision String, read the battery pack ID and firmware, or ask the serial pipe for them (see Firmware gates).
    5. Subscribe to mode, odometer, speed, battery remaining and charging, plus lights status where it exists.

    After a disconnect the app retries in 1 s. After a missing characteristic or a failed notification setup it retries in 10 s. Before reconnecting it refreshes Android's cached GATT table.

    The vendor account was optional ("Not now"). It was needed only for firmware updates and ride sharing, and it can no longer be created.

    Factory reset: unknown. No vendor article or community source in the research describes a board-side reset that clears phone bonds, the board name or settings. The spec records applicable: "unknown". Do not experiment with button combinations on a board you intend to ride.

    Rebinding: there is no network membership. A new phone bonds after the board is put back into pairing mode. Nothing needs the old phone.

    Pairing¶

    Property Value
    Pairing required Yes. The vendor app always bonds before connecting. Whether the board refuses an unbonded central is not recorded
    Security mode unknown. The app supplies no PIN and lets the OS finish the bond, which points to Just Works
    Bonding required
    PIN source None requested by the vendor app
    One central at a time Unknown
    One bond at a time Unknown
    Confidence medium

    Entering pairing mode: with the board on, press its power button five times. The button LED flashes blue, and the advertisement's pairing flag is set. This comes from the vendor's archived "How to Update Your Board's Software" article and the app's own prompts. How long the window stays open is not recorded. The same five presses also start remote pairing.

    Unpairing: forget the board in the phone's Bluetooth settings. No board-side way to drop a bond is documented.

    Recovery: close the vendor app on any other phone that is in range, forget the board in this phone's Bluetooth settings, re-enter pairing mode and bond again. 2nd-gen, Mini, Plus and Stealth boards accept the app and the remote at the same time. A 1st-gen board takes one or the other.

    Protocol Summary¶

    Generations¶

    Advertised service App's label Covers Confidence
    7dc55a86-c61f-11e5-9912-ba0be0483c18 "V2" 2nd-gen Dual/Dual+, Plus, Stealth, Mini S, Mini X; the Rev by the app's logic reported
    da2b84f1-6279-48de-bdc0-afbea0226079 "V1" 1st-gen boards. This is BlueRadios' stock BRSP serial-port service reported
    ea32b817-d410-42e2-848a-1218201468fc scooter Boosted Rev, checked on top of the V2 match by a helper the examined release never calls reported

    The BRSP identification is what ties the first-generation boards to this service: da2b84f1 is the 1st-gen serial pipe. It still wants a look at real hardware. Never match on da2b84f1 alone. It is a generic module service that unrelated products also carry.

    BLE Services¶

    UUID Name Placement evidence
    7dc55a86-c61f-11e5-9912-ba0be0483c18 Vehicle service Advertised. The 7dc5… characteristics share its base, so placement is probable
    65a8eaa8-c61f-11e5-9912-ba0be0483c18 Battery service Owners' own reads with a BLE explorer (community FAQ)
    ea32b817-d410-42e2-848a-1218201468fc Lights service Inferred from the shared base
    0000180a-0000-1000-8000-00805f9b34fb Device Information Owners' own reads (Firmware Revision String)
    da2b84f1-6279-48de-bdc0-afbea0226079 BlueRadios BRSP (1st gen) Identification only

    The app never names a service. It finds every characteristic by UUID across the whole table, so a client should do the same.

    Vehicle characteristics (7dc5xxxx-c61f-11e5-9912-ba0be0483c18)¶

    All values are little-endian.

    Characteristic Properties Layout Meaning
    7dc5bb39-… vehicle_id_name read, write text, NUL-padded Read: the board's name/identifier, trailing NULs stripped. Write: rename (below)
    7dc59643-… vehicle_model read u8 Model code, see Hardware. Selects the scales below
    7dc55dec-… vehicle_modes_count read u8 How many ride modes the firmware offers, from Beginner
    7dc55f22-… vehicle_mode read, write, notify u8 0 Beginner, 1 Eco, 2 Expert, 3 Pro, 4 Hyper. The app shows these as 1–5, and any other value as Eco
    7dc56594-… vehicle_odometer read, notify u32 LE Lifetime count × per-model factor = miles
    7dc56b34-… vehicle_speed read, notify s16 LE Count × per-model factor = mph. Negative values are shown as 0
    7dc56bfc-… vehicle_power unknown unknown Known to the app, never read
    7dc5c19d-… vehicle_units read, write u8 0 US (mi, mph), 1 metric (km, km/h). Meaningful on the Rev's display
    7dc5c201-… motor_driver_serial read bytes Shown by the app as uppercase hex
    7dc5c202-… motor_driver_firmware read 3 × u8 Printed as byte0.byte1.byte2
    58856524-0065-11e6-8d22-5e5517507c66 serial_command read, write, notify text lines Firmware-update pipe (below). Parent service unknown
    58856525-0065-11e6-8d22-5e5517507c66 serial_auth read u8 Non-zero first byte = pipe unlocked

    Per-model scales¶

    Model code Odometer (mi per count) Speed (mph per count)
    1, 2, 5, 6 (Dual, Dual+, Mini S, Mini X) and 0 3.6128e-5 (≈ 5.8 cm) 0.0028409092 (1/352 mph = 0.05 in/s)
    3, 4 (Plus, Stealth) 3.8386e-5 (≈ 6.2 cm) 0.0028409092
    7 (Rev) 6.2137273665e-4 (1 m) 0.00223694 (1 mm/s)

    The Plus/Stealth odometer factor is 1.0625 times the others, the ratio of 85 mm to 80 mm wheels. That points to a wheel-rotation count, but it is an inference.

    Worked examples, which the spec carries as test vectors:

    Characteristic Model Bytes Decoded
    speed 4 (Stealth) c0 0d 3520 → 10.0 mph
    speed 7 (Rev) 10 27 10000 → 22.37 mph (10 m/s)
    odometer 1 (Dual) 40 42 0f 00 1,000,000 → 36.128 mi
    odometer 3 (Plus) 40 42 0f 00 1,000,000 → 38.386 mi
    odometer 7 (Rev) 40 42 0f 00 1,000,000 → 621.37 mi (1000 km)

    Battery characteristics (65a8xxxx-c61f-11e5-9912-ba0be0483c18)¶

    Characteristic Properties Layout Meaning
    65a8eeae-… battery_remaining read, notify u8 State of charge, %
    65a8f5d4-… battery_charging read, notify u8 0 = not charging, non-zero = charging
    65a8f3c2-… battery_capacity read u32 LE Raw capacity count, physical unit unknown. The app's range factor is raw / 2,400,000 below 3,000,000 and raw / 2,000,000 at or above it (the extended-range pack)
    65a8f834-… battery_id read bytes Pack ID, shown as uppercase hex
    65a8f833-… battery_firmware read 3 × u8 Printed as byte0.byte1.byte2

    The pack ID and firmware characteristics exist from ESC firmware v1.5.0.

    Lights characteristics (ea32xxxx-d410-42e2-848a-1218201468fc)¶

    These drive the Rev's built-in lights and the Boosted Beams head and brake lights for boards (Beams support arrived with ESC 2.7.x, per the community). They are absent on a vehicle without lights support.

    Characteristic Properties Layout Meaning
    ea32dcac-… lights_status read, write, notify u8 0 off, 1 on. 2 "automatic" is in the app's value set but never offered
    ea32b761-… lights_default_mode read, write u8 1 = lights on at power-up, 0 = only when switched on
    ea326b96-… lights_brightness read, write u8 0–100 %. The app writes multiples of 5
    ea324d8c-… brake_lights_pattern read, write u8 1 = blink when braking, 0 = steady

    Device Information¶

    Characteristic Meaning
    00002a26-0000-1000-8000-00805f9b34fb Firmware Revision String ESC firmware, vX.Y.Z. The app's version gates use it
    00002a25-0000-1000-8000-00805f9b34fb Serial Number String Known to the app as the scooter serial, never read

    Commands¶

    Every write is a plain GATT write with no preceding handshake, no framing and no checksum.

    Command Characteristic Bytes Notes
    set_ride_mode 7dc55f22-… [mode], 0–4 advanced. Only indices below modes_count, only while stationary. Read first to be able to undo
    set_units 7dc5c19d-… [0] US / [1] metric
    rename_vehicle 7dc5bb39-… raw UTF-8, no terminator Must not be blank. Maximum length not recorded
    set_lights_power ea32dcac-… [0] / [1] The remote's double press also switches the lights. That it is the same state is an inference
    set_lights_startup ea32b761-… [0] / [1]
    set_lights_brightness ea326b96-… [0..100] Percent
    set_brake_light_pattern ea324d8c-… [0] steady / [1] blink

    The app's mode picker shows these top speeds, which are its own display constants: Beginner 18 km/h, Eco 25, Expert 32, Pro 35, Hyper 38 (Rev: 19.3, 29, 38.6). The firmware enforces the real caps. They vary by board, and the vendor's store screenshot of a 3rd-gen board shows 22/26/32/35/39 km/h. How many modes a board offers is in modes_count, which varies by model and firmware. The app also computes a range estimate from charge, the capacity factor, the mode and rider settings. The spec's protocol_details.range_estimate holds the formula and coefficients.

    Advertisement¶

    The first manufacturer-specific AD structure is read before connecting. Offsets count from the first byte of the AD value.

    Bytes Meaning
    0–1 Flags where a company ID would sit. Bit 0x02 of byte 0 = pairing mode. Do not filter on these bytes
    2 Firmware patch
    3 Firmware minor
    4 Firmware major. The app shows v<major>.<minor>.<patch>

    Example: 02 00 02 07 02 is a board in pairing mode on v2.7.2. The app also takes a 4-byte motor-driver serial from raw scan-record bytes 34–37 (reversed, as hex). That position depends on Android's scan-record buffer and is unverified on air.

    Firmware gates¶

    Version Read from Effect in the vendor app
    < v1.4.2 Firmware Revision String No battery pack ID or firmware at all
    v1.4.2 up to v1.5.0 Firmware Revision String Pack ID and firmware requested over the serial pipe (25 s timeout)
    ≥ v1.5.0 Firmware Revision String Pack ID and firmware characteristics read. Serial-pipe writes framed (not mapped)
    ≥ v1.6.0 Firmware Revision String Serial pipe locked behind a challenge answered by Boosted's servers
    < v2.0.0 advertisement Phone bond deleted and redone before every connection

    Serial pipe and firmware updates¶

    The vendor app updated firmware over 58856524/58856525 with Boosted's own bootloader protocol, not Nordic DFU. The app bundles no Nordic DFU or MCUmgr library. The images came from Boosted's servers. Commands are lines of text ending in CR, and replies arrive as notifications ending in CRLF. One of them is a version query, ALLFW. From ESC firmware v1.5.0 the app frames its writes. From v1.6.0 it must first read a challenge from 58856524-0065-11e6-8d22-5e5517507c66, send it with the board ID to Boosted's servers, and write the answer back. Those servers are gone. The framing and the bootloader frames are deliberately not documented here. A half-flashed ESC is a board nobody can ride.

    1st-gen boards (BRSP)¶

    The service da2b84f1-6279-48de-bdc0-afbea0226079 is BlueRadios' serial port. Its characteristics carry BlueRadios' published roles: info 99564a02-dc01-4d3c-b04e-3bb1ef0571b2, mode a87988b9-694c-479c-900e-95dfa6c00a24, RX (app to board) bf03260c-7205-4c25-af43-93b1c299d159, TX (board to app, indicate) 18cda784-4bd3-4370-85bb-bfed91ec86af, RTS flow control fdd6b4d3-046d-4330-bdec-1fd0c90cb43b, and CTS 0a1934f5-24b8-4f13-9842-37bb167c6aff, which the app names but never uses. The byte stream over RX/TX is not mapped. The vendor's 2014 insert gives the 1st-gen modes: Dual has Beginner 11 mph, Eco 16 mph and Expert 20 mph, and Dual+ adds Pro at 22 mph. On these boards the mode is cycled with three presses of the remote's button.

    Troubleshooting¶

    Symptom Cause
    Phone keeps asking to pair, or connects once then refuses Firmware older than v2.0.0 (the app worked around a bonding defect by re-bonding every time), or another phone still holds the connection. Forget the board, re-enter pairing mode, bond again
    Battery pack ID and firmware never appear ESC firmware older than v1.5.0. The details come over the serial pipe on v1.4.2 up to v1.5.0, and not at all before
    Extended-range pack flashes its power button white The board needs the software update that activates extended-range packs, and that update came from the dead servers
    The vendor app shows Eco for an odd mode byte The app shows any value outside 0–4 as Eco. Read the raw byte
    Hyper (mode 5) has vanished An ESC fault described by the owner community, which locks out the fifth mode after connecting to the vendor app. It is fixed by ESC work, not over this link
    Firmware update impossible The images and, from v1.6.0, the serial-pipe challenge both depended on Boosted's servers

    Remaining unknowns¶

    • Nothing has been checked on hardware. That includes the generation mapping, the Rev's advertised services (the app's scan filters imply it advertises 7dc55a86 too) and the parent services of the vehicle, lights and serial characteristics.
    • The pairing association model, whether the board enforces the bond, how many bonds it keeps, and how long the pairing window lasts.
    • The unit of the capacity count, the lengths of the serial and pack ID values, the maximum board-name length, and whether a rename changes the advertised name.
    • The layout of 7dc56bfc (power), whether boards expose 7dc5c19d (units), and which boards expose the lights characteristics.
    • The 1st-gen BRSP payload, the serial-pipe framing and the bootloader protocol are unmapped, the last two on purpose.
